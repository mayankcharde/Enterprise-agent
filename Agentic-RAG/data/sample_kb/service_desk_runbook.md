Service Desk Runbook

For a locked account, verify the employee's identity, check the account status in
the identity system, and unlock it when the lockout policy allows. Escalate repeated
lockouts to the identity team.

For a VPN problem, confirm that the user has internet access, is using the current
VPN client, and has the correct company profile. Collect the client error and
timestamp before escalating.

For an outage, create or update the incident, record affected users and services,
notify the on-call engineer, and add a status update before closing the ticket.
